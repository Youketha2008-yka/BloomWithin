03 · LEARN & UNDERSTAND

PCOS awareness

Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal condition. Symptoms and experiences can vary from person to person.

Signs someone may notice

Select any signs you would like to learn more about. Selecting a sign does not mean you have PCOS.

What you can do next

If you are concerned about symptoms or menstrual changes, consider making an appointment with a qualified healthcare professional. They can assess your history and decide whether tests or further evaluation are appropriate.

A symptom list alone cannot confirm or rule out PCOS. Other conditions can cause similar symptoms.

04 · HEALTH ACROSS LIFE STAGES

Menopause care

Menopause is a natural life stage. The timing and experience differ, and support is available.

Common changes

Select any topics you want to understand better.

When to seek advice

Talk with a healthcare professional if symptoms affect everyday life, you are unsure what is causing a change, or you have bleeding after menopause. They can discuss suitable assessment and support options.

This site provides general education, not personalised treatment advice.
